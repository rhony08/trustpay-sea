3 Crucial Emergency Recovery Steps

1

Contact Your Bank's Fraud Department RIGHT NOW

Ask to be connected directly to the Fraud / Dispute Operations Team. State clearly: "I have just made an erroneous transfer to a confirmed scammer. Please create an emergency incident report and initiate inter-bank communication to freeze the destination account at the recipient bank."

2

File an Official Police Report (SPKT / Cybercrime Division)

Financial institutions legally require an official police incident report (Surat Tanda Penerimaan Laporan Polisi or national equivalent) to permanently lock the recipient mule account and preserve remaining funds for judicial restitution.

3
